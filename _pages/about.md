Welcome to my blog. This is a space for me to write about the things that interest me. You can expect to find a wide variety of topics including

- Mathematics
- Crypto
- Blockchain security
- Finance
- Physics
- Programming

I am currently working as a blockchain security researcher at [OpenZeppelin](https://www.openzeppelin.com/). I was previously the head of risk management at [OneSixtyTwo Digital Capital](https://162digitalcapital.com/), a hedge fund specializing in investing in digital assets.

I completed a PhD in Applied Mathematics under the supervision of <a href="http://www.rotman.uwo.ca/portfolio-items/corless/" target="_blank">Prof. Robert M. Corless</a> and <a href="http://www.csd.uwo.ca/~moreno/" target="_blank">Prof. Marc Moreno Maza</a> at [Western University](https://uwo.ca/). My thesis developed algorithms for computations on matrices with parameters. These algorithms were implemented in the [Maple](https://www.maplesoft.com/) computer algebra system and are available in the [ParametricMatrixTools](https://github.com/steventhornton/parametricmatrixtools) package. My thesis also laid ground work for research on Bohemian matrices. Further information on Bohemian matrices can be found at [BohemianMatrices.com](http://www.bohemianmatrices.com/). My thesis can be found [here](https://ir.lib.uwo.ca/etd/6069/).

When I'm not learning new things I enjoy running, biking, hiking, camping, woodworking, and rock climbing.
